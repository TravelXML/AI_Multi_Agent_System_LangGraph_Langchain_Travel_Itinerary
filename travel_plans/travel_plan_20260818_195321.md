## 4️⃣ TRANSPORT ON THE GROUND  

### 4.1 JR Pass vs Point‑to‑point  
- **Tokyo → Kyoto → Osaka → Tokyo** (total ≈ 1 200 km).  
- Full **7‑day JR Pass** = ¥29 500 ≈ ₹18 000.  
- **Separate tickets** (Shinkansen Nozomi/ Hikari) cost about **₹13 000** total for the three legs (Tokyo‑Kyoto ¥7 500, Kyoto‑Osaka ¥2 500, Osaka‑Tokyo ¥3 000).  

**Recommendation:** Use the **point‑to‑point** tickets (cheaper and you keep the JR Pass for any extra day‑trips, e.g., Nara or Himeji). Purchase tickets via **JR East/West online** or at the station on the day of travel.

### 4.2 City transport  
- Get a **Suica (Tokyo) / ICOCA (Kansai)** prepaid IC card at the airport (≈ ₹500).  
- Daily cap ≈ ₹1 200 (subway + bus).  
- Use it for airport shuttles (Narita Express, Kansai Airport Express Haruka).
