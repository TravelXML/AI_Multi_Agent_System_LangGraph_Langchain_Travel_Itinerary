## KEY BUDGET‑SAVING TIPS  

| Area | How to Save |
|------|-------------|
| **Flights** | • Use **budget carriers** (Scoot, AirAsia X) for a 1‑stop – can drop to ~₹55 000 if you tolerate a 4‑hour layover.<br>• Book on **Tuesday‑Wednesday** and set price alerts on Google Flights. |
| **Accommodation** | • Book **capsule hotels** or **hostels** in Tokyo (e.g., **Nine Hours Shinjuku**) – ~₹5 000/night.<br>• In Kyoto, consider **guesthouses** like **Guest House An** (~₹6 500/night). |
| **Transport** | • If you only need 2‑3 Shinkansen rides, a **JR West Kansai‑Kyoto Area Pass** (¥5 000 ≈ ₹2 800) + **Tokyo Wide Pass** (¥4 200 ≈ ₹2 400) can be cheaper than a full 7‑day JR Pass.<br>• Use **IC cards** (Suica/Pasmo) for city metros – cheaper than buying single tickets each time. |
| **Food** | • Breakfast is often **included** in budget hotels; otherwise grab onigiri or convenience‑store meals (~₹300).<br>• Lunch at **ramen shops** or **bento boxes** from supermarkets (≈ ₹800‑₹1 200).<br>• Dinner at **izakayas** off the main tourist streets (cheaper). |
| **Attractions** | • Many temples/shrines are **free** (Senso‑ji, Fushimi Inari).<br>• Purchase a **Kansai Thru Pass** (¥4 300 ≈ ₹2 400) for Osaka/Kyoto day‑trips – covers buses & subways.<br>• Look for **combined tickets** (e.g., Kyoto Sightseeing Pass). |
| **Misc** | • Get a **local SIM** (e.g., **Sakura Mobile**) – ~₹1 200 for 7 days data.<br>• Pack a **reusable water bottle** and a **compact umbrella** (rain forecast). |
