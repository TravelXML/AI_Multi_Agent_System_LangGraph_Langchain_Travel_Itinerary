## DETAILED DAY‑BY‑DAY GUIDE  

### **Day 1 – Arrival in Tokyo**  
* **Flight**: Arrive at Narita 08:00 – 09:30 (local time).  
* **Transfer**: Keisei Skyliner (¥2 500) to Ueno → JR Yamanote to Shinjuku (¥200).  
* **Check‑in**: Hotel Gracery Shinjuku – modern capsule‑style rooms, great location, free Wi‑Fi.  
* **Lunch**: Asakusa’s **Daikokuya Tempura** (≈¥1 200).  
* **Afternoon**: Walk Senso‑ji, browse Nakamise‑dōri (souvenirs, snacks).  
* **Evening**: Tokyo Skytree (ticket ¥2 100). Night view of the city is spectacular.  
* **Dinner**: Street‑food stalls around Skytree (yakitori, yakisoba – ¥1 000).  

> **Travel tip** – Buy a **2‑day Tokyo Metro Pass** (¥1 500 ≈ ₹1 000) on day 1; it covers all subway rides until the evening of day 2.

---

### **Day 2 – Shibuya, Harajuku & Shinjuku**  
* **Morning**: Meiji Shrine (free) – walk through Yoyogi Park.  
* **Harajuku**: Takeshita‑dori for quirky fashion, crepes (¥500).  
* **Lunch**: Shibuya **Uobei Sushi** (conveyor‑belt sushi, ¥1 200).  
* **Afternoon**: Shibuya Crossing, Hachikō statue, then head to **Shibuya Sky** (¥2 300).  
* **Evening**: Explore Shinjuku Golden Gai – tiny bars, great for a cheap drink (¥800).  
* **Dinner**: Yakitori Alley (Omoide Yokocho) – ¥1 500.  

---

### **Day 3 – Hakone Day‑Trip (Mt. Fuji Region)**  
* **Early**: JR Shinkansen to Odawara (covered by JR Pass).  
* **Hakone Free Pass** (¥4 000) – includes transport, rope‑way, lake cruise.  
* **Lake Ashi**: Pirate‑ship cruise (¥1 000).  
* **Owakudani**: Volcanic valley, try black eggs (¥500).  
* **Onsen**: Public bath at **Hakone Kowakien** (¥1 200) – perfect for relaxing after a day of walking.  
* **Return** to Tokyo by evening.  

> **Why a day‑trip?**  It gives you a glimpse of Mt. Fuji and hot‑springs without the extra night‑stay cost.

---

### **Day 4 – Tokyo → Kyoto (Cultural Heart)**  
* **Morning**: Board the **Hikari** Shinkansen (7:30 am) – 2 h 15 m.  
* **Check‑in**: Hotel Mystays Kyoto Shijo (central, close to bus terminal).  
* **Lunch**: Gion’s **Gion Tanto** (vegetarian‑friendly udon, ¥1 200).  
* **Afternoon**: Walk the historic **Higashiyama** district, visit **Kiyomizu‑dera** (¥400).  
* **Evening**: Stroll **Maruyama Park**, enjoy cherry‑tree lights (if still in season) – free.  
* **Dinner**: Small izakaya in Gion (¥2 000).  

---

### **Day 5 – Arashiyama & Northern Kyoto**  
* **Morning**: JR Sagano Line to **Arashiyama** (JR Pass). Visit **Bamboo Grove** (free) and **Tenryū‑ji** (¥500).  
* **Lunch**: Riverside soba at **Yudofu Sagano** (≈¥1 300).  
* **Afternoon**: Take the **Hozu River boat** (optional, ¥1 200) or walk to **Kinkaku‑ji** (Golden Pavilion, ¥400).  
* **Evening**: Return to central Kyoto, relax at hotel.  
* **Dinner**: **Pontocho** alley – try a modest kaiseki set (≈¥2 500).  

---

### **Day 6 – Osaka – Food Capital**  
* **Morning**: JR rapid train to Osaka (≈15 min). Check‑in at **Hotel Sunroute Osaka Namba**.  
* **Umeda Sky Building**: Floating Garden Observatory (¥1 500).  
* **Lunch**: **Dotonbori** – takoyaki (¥600) + okonomiyaki (¥1 200).  
* **Afternoon**: Visit **Osaka Castle** (¥600) & its park.  
* **Evening**: Shopping in **Shinsaibashi** & **Namba** – pick up souvenirs.  
* **Dinner**: Street‑food crawl in Dotonbori (yakitori, kushikatsu – ¥1 500).  

> **Transport tip** – Purchase a **1‑day Osaka Subway Pass** (¥800) for unlimited rides.

---

### **Day 7 – Departure – Osaka → Tokyo → Home**  
* **Morning**: Quick visit to **Umeda**’s **Hankyu Department Store** for last‑minute gifts.  
* **Check‑out** → Take the **JR Haruka Express** (covered by JR Pass) to **Kansai International Airport**.  
* **Flight**: Kansai → Narita (if you flew into Narita) → connect to Delhi/Mumbai (≈9 h).  

*If you arrived in Tokyo (Narita) and are departing from Narita, simply take the **Narita Express** (JR Pass) back to the airport.*

## DETAILED DAY‑BY‑DAY GUIDE  

### **Day 1 – Arrival in Tokyo**  
* **Flight**: Arrive at Narita 08:00 – 09:30 (local time).  
* **Transfer**: Keisei Skyliner (¥2 500) to Ueno → JR Yamanote to Shinjuku (¥200).  
* **Check‑in**: Hotel Gracery Shinjuku – modern capsule‑style rooms, great location, free Wi‑Fi.  
* **Lunch**: Asakusa’s **Daikokuya Tempura** (≈¥1 200).  
* **Afternoon**: Walk Senso‑ji, browse Nakamise‑dōri (souvenirs, snacks).  
* **Evening**: Tokyo Skytree (ticket ¥2 100). Night view of the city is spectacular.  
* **Dinner**: Street‑food stalls around Skytree (yakitori, yakisoba – ¥1 000).  

> **Travel tip** – Buy a **2‑day Tokyo Metro Pass** (¥1 500 ≈ ₹1 000) on day 1; it covers all subway rides until the evening of day 2.

---

### **Day 2 – Shibuya, Harajuku & Shinjuku**  
* **Morning**: Meiji Shrine (free) – walk through Yoyogi Park.  
* **Harajuku**: Takeshita‑dori for quirky fashion, crepes (¥500).  
* **Lunch**: Shibuya **Uobei Sushi** (conveyor‑belt sushi, ¥1 200).  
* **Afternoon**: Shibuya Crossing, Hachikō statue, then head to **Shibuya Sky** (¥2 300).  
* **Evening**: Explore Shinjuku Golden Gai – tiny bars, great for a cheap drink (¥800).  
* **Dinner**: Yakitori Alley (Omoide Yokocho) – ¥1 500.  

---

### **Day 3 – Hakone Day‑Trip (Mt. Fuji Region)**  
* **Early**: JR Shinkansen to Odawara (covered by JR Pass).  
* **Hakone Free Pass** (¥4 000) – includes transport, rope‑way, lake cruise.  
* **Lake Ashi**: Pirate‑ship cruise (¥1 000).  
* **Owakudani**: Volcanic valley, try black eggs (¥500).  
* **Onsen**: Public bath at **Hakone Kowakien** (¥1 200) – perfect for relaxing after a day of walking.  
* **Return** to Tokyo by evening.  

> **Why a day‑trip?**  It gives you a glimpse of Mt. Fuji and hot‑springs without the extra night‑stay cost.

---

### **Day 4 – Tokyo → Kyoto (Cultural Heart)**  
* **Morning**: Board the **Hikari** Shinkansen (7:30 am) – 2 h 15 m.  
* **Check‑in**: Hotel Mystays Kyoto Shijo (central, close to bus terminal).  
* **Lunch**: Gion’s **Gion Tanto** (vegetarian‑friendly udon, ¥1 200).  
* **Afternoon**: Walk the historic **Higashiyama** district, visit **Kiyomizu‑dera** (¥400).  
* **Evening**: Stroll **Maruyama Park**, enjoy cherry‑tree lights (if still in season) – free.  
* **Dinner**: Small izakaya in Gion (¥2 000).  

---

### **Day 5 – Arashiyama & Northern Kyoto**  
* **Morning**: JR Sagano Line to **Arashiyama** (JR Pass). Visit **Bamboo Grove** (free) and **Tenryū‑ji** (¥500).  
* **Lunch**: Riverside soba at **Yudofu Sagano** (≈¥1 300).  
* **Afternoon**: Take the **Hozu River boat** (optional, ¥1 200) or walk to **Kinkaku‑ji** (Golden Pavilion, ¥400).  
* **Evening**: Return to central Kyoto, relax at hotel.  
* **Dinner**: **Pontocho** alley – try a modest kaiseki set (≈¥2 500).  

---

### **Day 6 – Osaka – Food Capital**  
* **Morning**: JR rapid train to Osaka (≈15 min). Check‑in at **Hotel Sunroute Osaka Namba**.  
* **Umeda Sky Building**: Floating Garden Observatory (¥1 500).  
* **Lunch**: **Dotonbori** – takoyaki (¥600) + okonomiyaki (¥1 200).  
* **Afternoon**: Visit **Osaka Castle** (¥600) & its park.  
* **Evening**: Shopping in **Shinsaibashi** & **Namba** – pick up souvenirs.  
* **Dinner**: Street‑food crawl in Dotonbori (yakitori, kushikatsu – ¥1 500).  

> **Transport tip** – Purchase a **1‑day Osaka Subway Pass** (¥800) for unlimited rides.

---

### **Day 7 – Departure – Osaka → Tokyo → Home**  
* **Morning**: Quick visit to **Umeda**’s **Hankyu Department Store** for last‑minute gifts.  
* **Check‑out** → Take the **JR Haruka Express** (covered by JR Pass) to **Kansai International Airport**.  
* **Flight**: Kansai → Narita (if you flew into Narita) → connect to Delhi/Mumbai (≈9 h).  

*If you arrived in Tokyo (Narita) and are departing from Narita, simply take the **Narita Express** (JR Pass) back to the airport.*
