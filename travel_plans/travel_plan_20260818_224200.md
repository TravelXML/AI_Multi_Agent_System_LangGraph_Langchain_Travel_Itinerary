- **Itinerary**: Toshogu Shrine (¥1,300) → Shoyoen Garden (free) → Kegon Falls (free, ¥550 elevator) → Lake Chuzenji (free lakeside walk).

- **Cost**: Transport ¥2,520 | Food ¥1,400 | Activities ¥1,300 | **Total: ₹2,900**

- **How**: Odakyu Romancecar from Shinjuku (round-trip ¥2,280) + Hakone Tozan Train (¥720 one-way). *Budget tip*: Skip Romancecar—use local trains (¥1,400 round-trip).  
- **Itinerary**: Hakone-Yumoto → Hakone Open-Air Museum (¥1,600) → Lake Ashi pirate ship (¥1,000) → Owakudani (free, volcanic views).  
- **Food**: Hakone-yama soba (¥900) → Sweet potato snack (¥300).

   - ❌ **Peak-season hotels**: Avoid Ginza/Roppongi (¥12,000+/night); stick to Asakusa/Ueno/Shinjuku.  
   - ❌ **Tourist restaurants**: Skip Shibuya Crossing or Times Square-style spots—walk 2 blocks back for 50% cheaper meals.

   - Fly **Delhi (DEL) → Tokyo Narita (NRT)** on **Tuesday** (cheapest day); return **Thursday**. Use **Scoot** (via Singapore) for ₹45k–50k RT if flexible.
