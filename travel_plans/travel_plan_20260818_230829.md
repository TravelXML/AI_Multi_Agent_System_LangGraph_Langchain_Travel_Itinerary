## Practical Tips  

1. **Visas** – Indian passport holders enjoy visa‑free entry for up to 90 days for tourism. No visa fee needed.  
2. **Currency** – Carry some yen (≈ ¥5,000‑¥10,000) for small vendors; most places accept IC cards (Suica/Pasmo) and credit cards.  
3. **Language** – Signage in major tourist areas is bilingual (Japanese/English). Translation apps (Google Translate, VoiceTra) help in local eateries.  
4. **Connectivity** – Rent a pocket‑Wi‑Fi at the airport (≈ ₹400/day) or buy a prepaid SIM (≈ ₹1,500 for 5 GB).  
5. **Luggage** – Most budget hotels allow 1‑2 kg free storage; use coin lockers at stations (¥300‑¥600 per day) for day‑trip bags.  
6. **Etiquette** – Speak softly on trains, avoid eating while walking, and rinse hands before entering shrines/temples.  
7. **Weather (late August)** – Expect warm, humid temps (25‑33 °C) with occasional showers. Pack light clothing, a compact umbrella, and comfortable walking shoes.  

Enjoy your Tokyo adventure – a perfect blend of tradition, pop‑culture, and modern convenience, all comfortably within a ₹2 lakh budget! Safe travels.
