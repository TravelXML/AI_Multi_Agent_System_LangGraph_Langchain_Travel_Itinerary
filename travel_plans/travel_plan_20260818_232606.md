## 8. Practical Tips & Money‑Saving Hacks  

| Tip | Why it helps |
|-----|--------------|
| **Fly mid‑week (Tue‑Thu)** & avoid Japanese holidays (late Mar‑early Apr, late Apr‑early May, Jul‑Aug, mid‑Dec‑early Jan). | Flights can be 20‑40 % cheaper. |
| **Use Suica/Pasmo** – top‑up ¥ 2,000‑¥ 3,000 and get refund of deposit at the end. | Saves on buying single tickets each ride. |
| **Convenience‑store breakfast** (onigiri, sandwich, coffee) – ¥ 300‑¥ 500 per person. | Cuts breakfast cost dramatically vs. hotel buffet. |
| **Lunch at ramen/gyudon chains** (Yoshinoya, Sukiya, Matsuya) – ¥ 600‑¥ 900. | Filling, tasty, cheap. |
| **Dinner at izakaya with “nomihōdai” (all‑you‑can‑drink) set** – ¥ 1,500‑¥ 2,000 per person for 2‑hour drink + small plates. | Good value if you enjoy a few drinks. |
| **Free attractions** – Meiji Shrine, Imperial Palace Gardens, Yoyogi Park, Shinjuku Gyoen (¥ 500), Sumida River walk. | Fill itinerary without spending. |
| **Luggage forwarding (takuhaibin)** – send suitcase from hotel to airport (≈ ¥ 2,000 per bag). | Travel light on trains, avoid excess‑baggage fees on LCCs. |
| **Travel insurance** – check if your credit card provides complimentary coverage; otherwise, a basic ₹ 500‑₹ 1,000 plan is sufficient for a short trip. | Protects against flight delays or medical emergencies. |
| **Currency** – carry ¥ 10,000‑¥ 15,000 cash for small stalls; the rest on card (most places accept Visa/Mastercard). | Some tiny shops/restaurants are cash‑only. |
