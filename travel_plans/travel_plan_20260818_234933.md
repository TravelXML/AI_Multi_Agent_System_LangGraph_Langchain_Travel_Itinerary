## 9. Practical Tips & Recommendations  

| Topic | Advice |
|-------|--------|
| **Visas** | Indian citizens need a **tourist visa** for Japan (single‑entry, 90‑day). Apply online via the Japan e‑Visa portal; processing ≈ 4‑5 business days; fee ≈ ₹1,000. |
| **Currency** | 1 JPY ≈ ₹0.55. Carry ¥10,000‑¥20,000 in cash for small shops; most places accept IC cards (Suica/Pasmo) and credit cards (Visa/Mastercard). |
| **Language** | Signage in major stations is bilingual (Japanese/English). Having a translation app (Google Translate) helps with menus. |
| **Connectivity** | Rent a pocket‑Wi‑Fi at Narita (≈ ₹400/day) or buy a prepaid SIM (e.g., b-mobile, ~₹1,500 for 5 GB). |
| **Etiquette** | Remove shoes when entering temples, ryokans, or some traditional restaurants; speak softly on public transport; queue orderly. |
| **Health** | No mandatory vaccinations for Japan; carry any personal medication; Japanese pharmacies (e.g., Matsumoto Kiyoshi) are well‑stocked. |
| **Safety** | Tokyo is among the safest cities worldwide; keep valuables in hotel safe; watch belongings in crowded areas. |
| **Budget Tracking** | Use a simple spreadsheet or travel‑budget app (Trail Wallet, Splid) to log daily spend; you’ll have ~₹30‑40 k cushion for upgrades or souvenirs. |
