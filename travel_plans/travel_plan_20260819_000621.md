## 6. Practical Tips & Recommendations  

| Topic | Advice |
|-------|--------|
| **Visa** | Indian tourists need an **e‑visa** (single‑entry, up to 30 days). Apply via the official Japan e‑visa portal (https://evisa.mofa.go.jp). Fee ≈ INR 450 per person. Processing time ~ 3‑4 business days. |
| **Travel Insurance** | A basic comprehensive plan (trip cancellation, medical, baggage) costs ≈ ₹1,500‑₹2,000 per person. Providers: ICICI Lombard, Bajaj Allianz, or global insurers like World Nomads. |
| **Currency** | 1 JPY ≈ ₹0.55 (rate Sep 2025). Carry ¥10,000‑¥15,000 cash for small vendors; most places accept IC cards and credit cards (Visa/MasterCard). Notify your bank of overseas usage. |
| **Language** | Signage in major stations is bilingual (Japanese/English). Learn a few greetings: “Konnichiwa” (hello), “Arigatō gozaimasu” (thank you), “Sumimasen” (excuse me/sorry). |
| **Connectivity** | Rent a pocket‑Wi‑Fi at Narita (≈ ₹400/day) or buy a prepaid SIM (e.g., b-mobile, IIJmio) – ~₹1,500 for 7 days data. Many hotels offer free Wi‑Fi. |
| **Etiquette** | Speak softly on public transport, avoid eating while walking, queue orderly, remove shoes when entering traditional inns or temples. |
| **Health** | No mandatory vaccinations for Japan from India. Carry any personal meds; pharmacies (ドラッグストア) are ubiquitous. |
| **Emergency** | Dial **110** for police, **119** for fire/ambulance. Keep your hotel’s address written in Japanese (can show to taxi driver). |
| **Packing** | September in Tokyo: average high 27‑30 °C, humidity moderate. Pack light‑weight clothing, a light jacket for evenings, comfortable walking shoes, umbrella (occasional showers). |
| **Apps** | • **Google Maps** (transit) <br>• **Japan Official Travel App** (tourist info) <br>• **HyperDia** or **JR East App** (train schedules) <br>• **Yahoo! Weather** (local forecasts) |
