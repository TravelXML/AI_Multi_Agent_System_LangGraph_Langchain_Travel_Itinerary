## 8️⃣ Practical Tips & Checklist  

| Item | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Visa** | Indian tourists need a **temporary visitor visa** (single‑entry, up to 90 days). Apply at the Japanese Embassy/Consulate in Delhi; fee ≈ ₹450. Processing 3‑5 working days. |
| **Passport** | Must be valid ≥ 6 months beyond travel date. |
| **Travel Insurance** | Recommended (medical + trip cancellation). Approx. ₹2,000 for 8‑day coverage. |
| **SIM / Pocket Wi‑Fi** | Buy a prepaid 5 GB SIM at Narita/Haneda (≈ ₹1,500) or rent pocket Wi‑Fi (≈ ₹2,500 for 7 days). |
| **Currency** | Exchange a small amount (¥5,000‑¥10,000) before departure for immediate expenses; withdraw yen from ATMs (7‑Eleven, Japan Post) for better rates. |
| **Language** | Basic Japanese greetings go a long way; most signage in major stations includes English. |
| **Etiquette** | No eating while walking, keep voice low on public transport, remove shoes when entering ryokan/temple interiors, cash still king in many small establishments. |
| **Weather (late Sep)** | Avg. high ≈ 27 °C, low ≈ 20 °C; occasional rain. Pack a light jacket, umbrella, comfortable walking shoes. |
| **Health** | No mandatory vaccinations; carry any personal meds and a copy of prescriptions. |
| **Emergency numbers** | Police 110, Fire/Ambulance 119, English‑speaking tourist help desk 03‑3201‑3331 (Japan National Tourism Organization). |
