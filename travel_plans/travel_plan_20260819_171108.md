## 6. Money‑Saving Tips (to keep you safely under the ₹ 200k ceiling)

| Tip | How it Helps |
|-----|--------------|
| **Book flights 2‑3 months ahead** (late June‑early July 2026) and set fare alerts on Google Flights/Skyscanner. | Locks in the low‑end ₹ 70k/person fare. |
| **Stay in a business hotel with breakfast included** (many offer a modest buffet). | Saves ~₹ 200‑₹ 300 per person per day on breakfast. |
| **Use convenience‑store meals** (7‑Eleven, FamilyMart, Lawson) for lunch/dinner – ¥500‑¥800 per meal, tasty and hygienic. | Cuts food cost vs. restaurant dining. |
| **Leverage free attractions** (temples, shrines, parks, Imperial Palace East Garden, many museums have free‑admission days). | Reduces entry‑fee spend. |
| **Purchase a prepaid Suica/Pasapo** and top up only as needed; avoid buying single‑ticket paper tickets (slightly cheaper). | Saves ~10 % on transit. |
| **Travel light** – no checked baggage fees on most full‑service carriers (Air India, JAL, ANA) for economy; brings down extra cost. | Avoids ¥2,000‑¥3,000 per bag fees. |
| **Consider a 7‑day JR Pass only if you plan multiple long‑distance trips** (e.g., Kyoto, Osaka). For a Tokyo‑only itinerary, point‑to‑point tickets are cheaper. | Prevents unnecessary spend. |
| **Travel insurance** – buy a basic policy online (≈ ₹ 800‑₹ 1,200 total) for peace of mind. | Small, predictable cost. |

## 7. Quick Checklist Before You Go

- **Passport:** Valid ≥ 6 months beyond 25 Sep 2026. Indian nationals get visa‑free tourism stay up to 90 days in Japan.  
- **Visa:** Not required for short stay; keep a copy of your passport and return flight ticket handy.  
- **Travel Insurance:** Purchase (medical, trip‑cancellation, baggage).  
- **Currency:** Exchange a small amount of INR → JPY (≈ ¥2,000) at the airport for immediate expenses; withdraw the rest from ATMs (7‑Eleven, Japan Post) – better rates.  
- **Power Adapter:** Japan uses Type A/B, 100 V. Bring a universal adapter.  
- **Language:** Basic Japanese greetings are appreciated; most signage in major tourist areas includes English.  
- **Health:** No special vaccinations required; carry any personal medication and a copy of the prescription.
